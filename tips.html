Master Your Studies

Proven techniques and habits to boost your learning and productivity.

⏲️

The Pomodoro Technique

Study for 25 minutes, then take a 5-minute break. After four sessions, take a longer break (15-30 mins). This keeps your mind fresh and prevents burnout.

🧠

Active Recall

Instead of just reading notes, test yourself. Close the book and try to explain the concept in your own words. This strengthens neural connections.

🔁

Spaced Repetition

Review your notes at increasing intervals (1 day, 3 days, 1 week, 1 month). This pattern helps move information from short-term to long-term memory.

💤

Optimize Your Sleep

Your brain processes information during sleep. Aim for 7-9 hours of quality sleep, especially before exams, to ensure peak cognitive performance.

🧘

Mindfulness & Breaks

Take 5-10 minutes to meditate or breathe deeply between study blocks. Reducing stress levels significantly improves concentration and retention.
